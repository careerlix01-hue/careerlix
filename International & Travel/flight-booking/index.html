✈️ Before Booking Your Flight

Compare the available flight timings, baggage allowance, fare conditions and cancellation rules before making your payment.

Flight fares and availability can change at any time. Always confirm the final fare and booking conditions on the airline's official website before payment.

⚠️ Flight Booking Safety

  • Use the airline's official website whenever possible for direct booking.
  • Check the website address carefully before entering personal or payment information.
  • Verify passenger names, travel dates, airports and baggage allowance before payment.
  • Keep your booking confirmation and PNR/e-ticket information safe.
  • Check cancellation, refund and rescheduling rules before completing the booking.
