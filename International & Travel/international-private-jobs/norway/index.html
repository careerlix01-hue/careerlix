Private-sector career information and employer verification guidance for Norway.

Private vacancies

Coming soon: Careerlix will publish individual private-sector vacancies only after the employer and vacancy source can be checked.

Applicants should apply through the employer's official careers page and confirm job title, location, eligibility, closing date and work-authorization requirements.

What to verify

  • Employer identity
  • Official careers page
  • Job reference and location
  • Application deadline
  • Work authorization requirements

For Indian applicants

Check whether the employer accepts international applicants and whether sponsorship or another work authorization route is available.

Safety note

Never pay an unknown recruiter for a job offer. Verify the vacancy directly with the employer before sharing sensitive documents.
