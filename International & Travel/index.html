🌎 Your International Journey Starts Here

Whether you are looking for an international government job, a private-sector opportunity, passport information, visa resources or flight booking assistance, Careerlix is designed to help you find the right starting point.

Always use official government, employer, airline or authorised service-provider websites for applications, payments and personal information.

📌 Important Safety Information

  • Always verify international job vacancies through the official employer or government recruitment website.
  • Never pay an unknown person or unofficial agent to guarantee a job or visa.
  • Use official government websites for passport and visa applications whenever available.
  • Verify visa, immigration and work authorization requirements before travelling.
  • Check flight booking details, cancellation policies and applicable travel requirements before making a payment.
