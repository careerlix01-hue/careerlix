🇮🇳 Official Visa Website

The official Indian Visa Online portal is indianvisaonline.gov.in. It provides Regular/Paper Visa and e-Visa services and is managed by the Bureau of Immigration, Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India.

Regular Visa applicants may need to submit the printed application and supporting documents to the concerned Indian Mission or Visa Application Center, depending on the applicable process.

⚠️ Important Safety Information

  • Use only the official indianvisaonline.gov.in website for Indian Visa services.
  • Do not pay unknown agents for emergency or express e-Visa services.
  • Never share your passport, personal or payment information with an unauthorized visa website.
  • Check the latest eligibility, documents, fees and visa requirements before applying.
  • For Regular Visa applications, also check the website of the concerned Indian Mission for location-specific requirements.
